Mission Parameters

Why the Programme was Started

Rural government school children frequently lack access to structured comprehension assets, personal career guidance pathways, and advanced personality development setups beyond normal textbook configurations. Granth Mitra fills this operational gap by mobilizing specialized college student bodies to act as direct field role models during weekly Saturday mentoring sessions, providing essential rural educational support and vibrant school engagement [dynamic-map].

Core Programme Objective

Granth Mitra systematically channels student leadership directly into underprivileged regional clusters to enhance foundational testing scores, optimize vocabulary literacy tracks, and configure digital skills competencies.
