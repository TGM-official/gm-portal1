Empowering Rural Education Through Youth Mentorship

Granth Mitra is an educational volunteer initiative of Akanksha Charitable Trust where college students dedicate their time to mentoring rural government school students through weekly learning sessions, reading activities, life skills, and community engagement to build a stronger society.
