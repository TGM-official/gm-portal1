GRANTH MITRA | ಗ್ರಂಥಮಿತ್ರ Initiative of Akanksha Charitable Trust
✨ Flagship Youth Initiative

Empowering Rural Education Through Youth Mentorship

Granth Mitra is an educational volunteer initiative of Akanksha Charitable Trust where college student clusters dedicate their time to mentoring rural government school students through weekly learning sessions, library setups, and community engagement tracks.
