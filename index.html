Mission Parameters

Why the Programme was Started

Rural government school children frequently lack access to structured comprehension assets, personal career guidance pathways, and advanced personality development setups beyond normal textbook configurations. Granth Mitra fills this operational gap by mobilizing specialized college student bodies to act as direct field role models during weekly Saturday mentoring sessions, providing essential rural educational support and vibrant school engagement.

Core Programme Objective

Granth Mitra systematically channels student leadership directly into underprivileged regional clusters to enhance foundational testing scores, optimize vocabulary literacy tracks, and configure digital skills competencies.

Field Sequence

The Structural Six-Step Engagement Track

Step 01

College Students Register

Eligible campus volunteers register under their local institutional units.

Step 02

Volunteer Orientation

Volunteers undergo validation tracks run directly by ACT core coordinators.

Step 03

Library or School Allocation

Mentors are systematically mapped to targeted village government schools.

Step 04

Weekly Mentoring Sessions

Execution of interactive vocabulary loops and science workshops on Saturdays.

Step 05

Activity Reporting

Task cards and metrics updates are logged directly to the separate backend sheet application.

Step 06

Community Impact & Recognition

Volunteers secure university credits and trust validation completion certificates.
